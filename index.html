Model boundaries

This is single-decree Paxos with two proposers and three or five acceptors. A value is chosen when a majority has accepted the same ballot/value. The observer derives this from the complete simulated vote history; a proposer learns it only after receiving a majority of acceptance acknowledgments.

Promises and accepted records are persistent only within the simulation. There is no disk database, actual network, proposer crash recovery, automatic retry, timeout, leader election, membership change, or replicated command log. The simulator supplies unique increasing ballot numbers; a real implementation needs a distributed uniqueness scheme. Restarting an acceptor never erases its promise or accepted record.

Safety is checked after every transition. Progress is not guaranteed under message loss, unavailable majorities, or competing attempts. Limits: 160 actions and 60 pending messages. Deliberately invalid actions are rejected atomically.

Majorities overlap. The history matters.
